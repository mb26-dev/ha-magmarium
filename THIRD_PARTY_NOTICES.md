# Third party data and notices

Magmarium does not ship any third party data. Everything below is downloaded at runtime by the integration and only cached locally.

| Data | Provider | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volcano alert levels (Level I-IV) and VONA notices for Indonesia | [MAGMA Indonesia](https://magma.esdm.go.id), Center for Volcanology and Geological Hazard Mitigation (PVMBG / CVGHM), Geological Agency of Indonesia | Public web pages, read every 10 minutes (alert levels every hour) |
| Weekly Volcanic Activity Report | [Smithsonian Institution Global Volcanism Program](https://volcano.si.edu) and the [USGS Volcano Hazards Program](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/VHP) | Public RSS feed, read every 6 hours |
| Volcanoes of the World (Holocene volcano list) | [Smithsonian Institution Global Volcanism Program](https://volcano.si.edu), Volcanoes of the World database | Names, positions and heights of all known volcanoes, read once a month |
| Volcano alert levels and aviation colour codes of US volcanoes | [USGS Volcano Hazards Program](https://volcanoes.usgs.gov), Hazard Notification System (HANS) public API | Read every hour |
| Aviation colour codes of Icelandic volcanoes | [Icelandic Met Office](https://en.vedur.is) (Veðurstofa Íslands), VONA notifications page | Read every hour |
| Volcanic alert levels of Japanese volcanoes | [Japan Meteorological Agency](https://www.jma.go.jp) | Public JSON of the JMA disaster prevention map, read every hour |
| Alert levels of the Italian volcanoes | [Dipartimento della Protezione Civile](https://mappe.protezionecivile.gov.it/it/mappe-e-dashboard-rischi/mappa-livelli-di-allerta-vulcani-italia/), data of the official map from [pcm-dpc/DPC-Mappe](https://github.com/pcm-dpc/DPC-Mappe), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | GeoJSON read every hour |
| VONA notices for Etna and Stromboli | [INGV Osservatorio Etneo](https://www.ct.ingv.it) (Istituto Nazionale di Geofisica e Vulcanologia) | Public list of notices read every 15 minutes; volcano, time and colour code are used, the PDF notices are only linked |
| Earthquakes in and around Italy | [INGV](https://terremoti.ingv.it) (Istituto Nazionale di Geofisica e Vulcanologia), FDSN event web service | Quakes M2+ of the last 7 days, read every 15 minutes |
| Volcanic ash SIGMETs | [AviationWeather.gov](https://aviationweather.gov), NOAA Aviation Weather Center (public domain) | International SIGMETs with hazard VA, read every 15 minutes |
| Earthquakes | [USGS Earthquake Hazards Program](https://earthquake.usgs.gov) (public domain) | GeoJSON feeds M4.5+ of the week and M2.5+ of the day, read every 15 minutes |
| Tsunami warnings | [NOAA Tsunami Warning Centers](https://www.tsunami.gov) PTWC and NTWC (public domain, US Government) and [BMKG InaTEWS](https://inatews.bmkg.go.id) (open data of BMKG) | Atom feeds of the warning centers and the BMKG earthquake lists with their tsunami potential, read every 5 minutes; only official bulletins are shown |
| Satellite hotspots and wildfires | [NASA FIRMS](https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov) (LANCE / EOSDIS, open data, citation requested) | Only with the user's own free map key: VIIRS NOAA-20 and S-NPP area queries around active volcanoes every 3 hours and one area around home every hour |
| Wildfire map tiles | [NASA GIBS](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/engage/open-data-services-software/earthdata-developer-portal/gibs-api) (NASA open data, no restrictions, attribution requested) | Loaded by the browser of the user while the wildfire card is shown; nothing is stored by the integration |
| English Wikipedia article per volcano | [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org) (CC0), property P1886 Smithsonian volcano ID | SPARQL query, read once a month; only the article titles are used |
| Volcano camera images and pages | [USGS AshCam](https://avo-volcview.wr.usgs.gov/ashcam-gui/webcam.html) (public domain), [GeoNet](https://www.geonet.org.nz/volcano/cameras) (GNS Science), [Japan Meteorological Agency](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/vois/data/obs/volcam/volcam.php), [MAGMA Indonesia](https://magma.esdm.go.id/v1/gunung-api/cctv), [INGV Osservatorio Etneo](https://www.ct.ingv.it) (Etna, Stromboli, Vulcano; the camera pages are read every 10 minutes because the image address changes with every picture) | Camera lists read every 6 hours; the images are loaded by the browser directly from the observatory, nothing is copied or stored |
| Winds aloft (850 to 250 hPa) for the ash drift simulation | [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), weather data licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | Hourly forecast winds at the places of volcanoes with an ash cloud or an eruption, read every hour, free non-commercial API without a key |
| Earth imagery | NASA Earth Observatory: [Blue Marble Next Generation](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/) by Reto Stöckli and [Black Marble 2016](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/earth-at-night/maps) | Day and night side of the globe, credit "NASA Earth Observatory" |

The volcano, lava and ash drawings are original artwork rendered by the card. Ash clouds and eruptions are illustrations of the official reports, not measurements or forecasts. The dashed drift track is a simple simulation from forecast winds at one point, not an official volcanic ash advisory. For aviation and safety decisions always use the official sources above.
